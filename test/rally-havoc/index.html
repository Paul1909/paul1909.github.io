Stages
Your car
Your car accelerates by itself. You only steer.
  • Phone / iPad: hold the left or right half of the screen. Hold both for the handbrake.
  • Keyboard: ← → (or A / D). ↓ or Space = handbrake. P = pause.
  • Curves push you outwards: steer early. Handbrake + steer = drift.
  • Near misses, drifts and jumps fill the TURBO bar. When it's full, you blast off.
  • Every hit costs damage. Too much and your car explodes. Wrenches repair you.
  • Ram rivals into traffic to wreck them. Red barrels explode.
  • Lights: red means cross traffic and people on the crossing. Pedestrians cost you 1000 points.
  • Finish in the top 3 to unlock the next stage.
