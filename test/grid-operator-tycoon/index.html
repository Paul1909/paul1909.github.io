📖 How to play

You run the power grid of a small country. Towns pay for electricity, and you spend it on plants, lines and everything that keeps them running. Each day on the clock stands for a whole month of bills.

🏗️ Build plants

  • Solar, wind: cheap power, no fuel, but only when the sun shines or the wind blows. Coasts and hills are windier, the south is sunnier (the map shows site quality while you place them).
  • Hydro dams sit on rivers by the hills. Their lakes fill with snowmelt in spring and run low in winter.
  • Gas peakers start in minutes. CCGTs take 2 hours, coal 8 hours and nuclear a day and a half, and they can only ramp so fast.
  • Batteries react instantly: they charge on cheap midday power and cover evening peaks and sudden plant trips.

🗼 String power lines

  • Tap Lines, tap a plant or town, then tap another. Tap empty ground to drop a substation as a junction.
  • Power flows by physics, not by your wishes: it splits over every parallel path, more of it on the shorter, higher-voltage ones.
  • A line over 100 % heats up (🔥) and trips after a while. Lines also trip in storms. If a town hangs on one line, a trip means a blackout. Loops keep the lights on (that's the N-1 rule).
  • Upgrade busy lines to 220 kV, or 380 kV after research.

⚡ Dispatch is automatic

Every few minutes the grid runs the cheapest plants first (the merit order), starts slow plants ahead of the evening peak and trades with neighbours at the spot price. If there isn't enough power, towns get rolling blackouts: fines, angry thoughts and lost customers. Keep a reserve!

😊 Towns

Towns think out loud, like park guests. They like reliable, cheap and clean power. They hate blackouts, high bills, smog from coal and pylons across their view. Happy towns grow and use more power. Your approval is the average mood.

💰 Money

Set the tariff, the maintenance level (fewer breakdowns, faster repairs) and the R&D budget in Money. Fuel and CO₂ cost money every hour, and the CO₂ price rises each year. Borrow if you need to, but if you're in the red for 4 months the bank takes over.

🎯 Scenarios

Each scenario has goals and a deadline. Meet every goal early for a gold medal. Big customers like data centres sometimes ask to be connected: be quick, they pay well.

🖐️ Controls

Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom. Tap anything to inspect it. ▶ starts the clock, 1× / 3× / 8× sets the speed. On a keyboard: Space pause, 1–3 speed, B build, L lines, Esc cancel.
