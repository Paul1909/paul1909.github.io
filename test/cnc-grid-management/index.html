      Field manual

      The job. Villages need power. You build plants, lay power lines to them and keep supply equal to demand, every hour of the day. Each MWh delivered pays credits. Blackouts make villages angry.

      Building. Tap a picture in the sidebar to start building. When it says READY, tap it again, then tap the map to place it (white squares = ok, red = blocked), then tap again or press PLACE. Plants must go near your base or next to a Substation. Tap a building picture while it builds to pause it; hold it to cancel.

      Power lines. Tap POWER LINE, then drag across the map from your base, plant or another line. Every tile costs credits. A village is connected when a line touches one of its houses. Two fingers pan the map while laying lines.

      The power bar (left of the pictures) shows what your plants can deliver right now; the white mark is demand. Green is fine, yellow is tight, red means blackouts. Low power slows construction and shuts down the radar and Tesla coils.

      Plants. Coal is cheap but slow, dirty and taxed for CO₂. Gas turbines start fast and run on AUTO. Wind and sun are free but follow the weather and the clock. Hydro must sit across the river. The SCADA Dome unlocks batteries, nuclear and Tesla coils and gives weather warnings.

      Tools. REPAIR fixes broken lines (instant, costs credits) and speeds up tripped plants. SELL demolishes for half the price back. POWER switches a plant on or off. Tapping a building also shows these buttons.

      Trouble. Blizzards and storms snap lines. Copper thieves walk in and strip pylons. Repair Trucks fix things for free; Patrol Jeeps and Tesla Coils deal with thieves. Plants sometimes trip offline. Build loops so one broken line can't black out a village.

      Controls. Drag to scroll, pinch or mouse wheel to zoom, tap the radar to jump. Select a truck or jeep and tap the ground to send it. Keyboard: Space pauses, Esc cancels.
