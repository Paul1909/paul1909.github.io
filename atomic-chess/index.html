💥 Atomic chess

Normal chess moves, with one explosive twist:

  • Every capture is an explosion. The captured piece, the capturing piece and every piece on the 8 surrounding squares are destroyed.
  • Pawns resist blasts. Pawns next to an explosion survive (unless they captured or were captured).
  • Win by blowing up the enemy king, either by capturing it or by a capture right next to it. Checkmate also wins.
  • Kings cannot capture — they would blow themselves up.
  • You may never blow up your own king, even if it would also blow up theirs.
  • Touching kings are safe: when the two kings stand next to each other, neither is in check.

Tap a piece, then tap where it goes, or drag it. While dragging onto a capture, the blast zone glows.
